## Why Choose a Commercial License?

The open-source GPLv3 license is ideal for free software, personal use, community projects, and open-source contributions. 

However, if your organization or product:
- Requires embedding or bundling this generator into proprietary or closed-source commercial software products.
- Requires redistributing compiled binaries without disclosing corresponding source code.
- Cannot comply with copyleft obligations under the GNU GPLv3 license.
- Requires commercial warranties, indemnification, custom cryptographic adaptations, or priority enterprise support.

Then a **Commercial License** is the appropriate choice.
